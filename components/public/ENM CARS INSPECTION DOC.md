# ENM Cars Inspection Checklist

## 1. Questions to Ask the Owner
Make sure the seller's story is realistic and consistent:
1. How long have you owned the car?
2. Why are you selling it?
3. Does everything work? Any problems?
4. Any service records? What have you done since owning the car?
5. What did you use the car for?
6. How many miles did you put on it while you owned it?
7. What car are you looking to get now? Why?

## 4. Physical Inspection

### 4.1 The Car Basics
- Verify trade-in value and NTSA ownership details.

### 4.2 The Body
- Check for rust, dents, scratches, or signs of repainting.

### 4.3 The Wheels
- Inspect tire condition, tread wear, and alignment.

### 4.4 The Interior
- Check upholstery, controls, and smell for any issues.

### 4.5 Under the Car
- **Leaks**: Near engine and rear differential.  
- **Rust**: On exhaust, frame, body, etc.  
- **Suspension**: Tie rods, ball joints, shocks, axle, etc.  
- **Frame Damage**: Signs of previous accidents or misalignment.  

### 4.6 Engine Inspection
#### **Before Startup**
- Bonnet opens easily?  
- Bonnet support/struts work?  
- Frame straight, no kinks?  
- Check for leaks (oil, coolant, transmission fluid).  
- Oil level, no metal shavings or froth.  
- Inspect coolant (when cold).  
- Hoses, belts, battery post, wires.

#### **Cold Startup**
- Any strange noises/smoke? (ticking, grinding, etc.)  
- Does it take too long to start?  
- Excessive motor movement (bad mounts)?  
- Strong pulses/smoke under oil cap?  
- Idle stability and exhaust smoke check.  

#### **Warm Startup**
- Any strange noises/smoke?  
- Bubbling in radiator reservoir (head gasket issue)?  

### 4.7 Interior Features Check
- **Seats**: Adjustability, damage, stains, smell.  
- **Carpet & Trunk**: Check under carpets for rust, dampness, spare tire, jack, and lug wrench.  
- **Electronics**:
  - Power windows/mirrors/locks  
  - Radio, AC/Heat, navigation, sunroof  
  - Parking sensors, emergency brake  
  - Dashboard lights, gauges, wipers  
  - Rearview mirror auto-dimming  

- **Headliner**: Check for sagging, water damage, burns.  
- **Wear & Tear**: Gas/brake pedal, steering wheel, shift knob.  

### 4.8 Exterior Inspection
- **Lights**: High/low beams, fog lights, turn signals, brake lights.  
- **Bodywork**: Check panel gaps, paint seams, bondo repairs.  
- **Tires**:
  - Inspect tread wear  
  - Check camber/toe angles  
  - Turn lock to lock for alignment issues

## 5. Test Drive
Perform a city and highway test drive.

### **Acceleration Test**
- **Regular Acceleration**: No hesitation or knocking.  
- **Hard Acceleration**: Smooth response.  

### **Turning Test**
- **Stationary**: Turn lock to lock (check for noises).  
- **Moving**: Perform full 360° turns.  
- **Sway Test**: Turn back and forth while moving (listen for knocks).  
- **Hard Turns**: Ensure stability and control.  

### **Brake Test**
- **Regular Braking**: Smooth, no pulling, noises, or vibrations.  
- **Hard Braking**: Activate ABS if possible.  
- **ABS Light Check**: Ensure it functions properly.  

### **Highway Test**
- Stop and go, bumps, turns.  
- Check for noises, vibrations, and smooth RPM transition.  
- Test cruise control functionality.  

### **City Driving Test**
- Observe noises, vibrations, and shifting performance.  
- Ensure smooth operation in stop-and-go traffic.  
- Scan engine codes again after the drive.

## Summary
This checklist provides a detailed step-by-step guide to thoroughly inspecting a used car before purchasing. It covers all major components, including the exterior, interior, engine, and on-road performance.
